RULES

Drop packets onto the network stacks, one column at a time.

If the width or height of an unbroken stack matches a packet's number, that packet disappears.

Firewalls [=] / [-] slow your hacking down. Making adjacent packets disappear breaks them down and reveals their numbers.

A new row of firewalls rises every 8 drops. Anything left above the ======= line after clears disconnects you.

Easy also previews the next packet, and shorter chains unlock hacks. Hard gives you fewer drops between firewalls the higher your score.

Click a column or press 1–7 to drop
