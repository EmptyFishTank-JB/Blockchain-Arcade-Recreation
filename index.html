RULES

Encrypted bits fall into the terminal. Drop each one into a column.

A bit decrypts when its number matches the length of the unbroken line it sits in, across or down. Decrypting can bring more bits down into new matches for a chain.

A decrypted bit scores 10 plus its number (a [4] is 14), multiplied by the chain. Blocks wiped out by exploits score a flat 10.

Encryption layers [=] rise from below every 8 drops. Decrypt a bit beside one to peel a layer off; the second peel ([-]) reveals the bit hidden underneath.

Keep your columns below the ======== line. If anything is still above it once the decrypting stops, the trace completes and you're caught.

On Easy and Normal, every 4 bits a single drop decrypts make a NIBBLE, worth 16 bonus points.

Easy shows the next bit, and shorter chains unlock exploits.

Hard is a full byte: an 8×8 terminal with bits up to 8, and new layers come faster as your score climbs. Every 8 bits a single drop decrypts make a BYTE, worth 256 bonus points.

Click a column or press 1–7 to drop

    DEV // EVERYTHING UNLOCKED

    EXPLOITS

    Chain 5 decrypts in one drop to unlock a random exploit. It waits in the exploit button (lower right): tap it to arm the exploit as your next drop, then drop it like a bit and it runs wherever it lands. Once armed, there's no taking it back.

    § WORM VIRUS

    Burrows down the column it lands in and wipes out every block there.

    + BUFFER OVERFLOW

    Floods memory: every bit goes up by 1. A bit pushed past 7 gets re-encrypted under two layers.

    ◈ TROJAN

    Slips in quietly, then wipes out every block touching the spot where it lands.

    ? RNG

    Scrambles every bit to a new random number.

    BITFLIP

    Turns every column upside down.

    # DICTIONARY ATTACK

    Throws every known key at the encryption: every layer on the board loses one level at once.

    @ KEYLOGGER

    Records the incoming stream: shows your next 3 bits for the next 10 drops.

    ⇆ PIVOT

    Moves sideways through the network: the column it lands in swaps places with the one beside it. Pick the column, then tap the neighbor (← or →) to swap with: once picked, you're committed. Edge columns swap with their only neighbor.

    ~ PACKET SNIFFER

    Taps the incoming stream: for your next 3 bits, tap CURRENT (or press ↑ / ↓) to choose each one's number.

    ! LOGIC BOMB

    Lands as a block counting down from 3. Three drops later it detonates, wiping out everything in the 5×5 around it.

    ◎ HONEYPOT

    Lands as a trap. When a bit next to it decrypts, every bit with that number within 2 cells of the trap decrypts too.

    _ BACKDOOR

    Opens a way in underneath: the entire bottom row is deleted, layers included, and everything drops by one.

    * RAINBOW TABLE

    Looks up the most common number on the board and decrypts every bit showing it.
