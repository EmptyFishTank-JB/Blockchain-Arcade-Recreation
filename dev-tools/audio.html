00 Dev Switches

For testing in this browser only. UNLOCK EVERYTHING works like owning Full Access (Hard, every track, theme and exploit) without touching your saved progress, and the game shows a DEV badge while it's on. While it's on, press and hold any exploit card in the game for 2 seconds to make it your next drop, slotted or not. The other two edit your saved progress so you can try levels, exploit slots and DECRYPTOR ranks quickly.

01 One-Shot Effects

Nine short synthesized sounds defined in sfx.js: eight ported from the ECHOES terminal audio compendium, plus the data burst made for decrypting bits. They're turned on and off with the SOUND button in the game; the buttons here play them regardless.

02 Music Tracks

The playlist tracks, each a 32-bar loop scheduled live by music.js. In the game, music starts on your first click or key press and the gear/speaker settings icon picks the track. Play one here and drag its intensity slider to hear the danger layers blend in.

03 Music Intensity

Whichever track is playing gets more intense as your tallest stack nears the red ======= line. The game sends the music a target from 0 to 1 every time the board redraws; the music eases toward it a little every 16th note, so layers fade in and out over a few seconds instead of switching. The track itself never restarts.

TALLEST STACKINTENSITYWHAT YOU HEAR
0–3 rows0%The track as written.
4 rows33%16th-note hi-hats come in and the tone starts brightening (layers from 5%).
5 rows67%The low end thickens: a kick on every beat in the theme, extra chugs and double-kick fills in SLEEP MODE (layers from 40%).
6+ rows100%The alarm layer: the tension saw in the theme, the square-wave alarm in SLEEP MODE (layers from 72%). Two rows from the line or closer.
Game over0%Eases back to calm behind the CONNECTION LOST screen.

It moves 6% of the remaining distance each 16th note: about two-thirds of the way in roughly 2 seconds on the theme (1.6 seconds on SLEEP MODE, which is faster), and fully settled after about 6 seconds. Each layer has its own entry point and fades in over the next 25% (the brightening layer grows across the whole range). Entry points sit just above the previous stack height's level, so each extra row brings in the next layer. The exact values are listed on each track above, read straight from the track's code, with live level bars while it plays.
