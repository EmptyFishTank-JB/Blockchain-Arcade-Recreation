AUDIO COMPENDIUM

Every sound in the game: the one-shot effects, both music tracks, and how the music intensifies near the red line. Nothing here is a recording. This page loads the game's own sfx.js, music-theme.js and music-sleep-mode.js, so every button plays exactly what the game plays.

01 One-Shot Effects

Nine short synthesized sounds defined in sfx.js: eight ported from the ECHOES terminal audio compendium, plus the data burst made for packet clears. They're turned on and off with the SOUND button in the game; the buttons here play them regardless.

02 Music Tracks

The playlist tracks, each a 32-bar loop scheduled live by music.js. In the game, music starts on your first click or key press and the speaker icon picks the track. Play one here and drag its intensity slider to hear the danger layers blend in.

03 Music Intensity

Whichever track is playing gets more intense as your tallest stack nears the red ======= line. The game sends the music a target from 0 to 1 every time the board redraws; the music eases toward it a little every 16th note, so layers fade in and out over a few seconds instead of switching. The track itself never restarts.

TALLEST STACKINTENSITYWHAT YOU HEAR
0–4 rows0%The track as written.
5 rows33%Danger layers start fading in, quietly.
6 rows67%Clearly busier and brighter.
7+ rows100%Full intensity: one row from the line (or already in the overflow row).
Game over0%Eases back to calm behind the CONNECTION LOST screen.

It moves 6% of the remaining distance each 16th note: about two-thirds of the way in roughly 2 seconds on the theme (1.6 seconds on SLEEP MODE, which is faster), and fully settled after about 6 seconds. Each track decides what its layers are — see the Intensity layers list on each track above.
